RAMix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

RAMix is a puzzle game developed by Raed Alkhaldi. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Authentication data: If you sign in with Apple or X (Twitter), we store your user ID, display name, and profile photo URL to identify you in multiplayer games. If you play as a guest, we use anonymous Firebase authentication with no personal data.

Game data: We store multiplayer room data (room codes, game progress, move counts, and solve times) to enable real-time multiplayer gameplay. This data is temporary and tied to individual game sessions. Your results in daily challenges, races and challenges against other players — including a record of your moves, which other players may race against — are shown to other players with your username. We also count the days you open the app and your current level, so we can see how the game is played.

Username: If you sign in with Apple, you choose a username that is stored in our database and visible to other players in multiplayer rooms.

Advertising attribution data: We use AppsFlyer to learn which of our ads led you to install RAMix. See Advertising Attribution below.

Tournament results: If you enter a tournament, we store your username, your solve times, the number of moves, and a record of the moves (used to verify that a winning solve is genuine). Your username and time appear on that tournament's public leaderboard.

Coins and ratings: Your coin balance, what you earned coins for and what you spent them on, and your rating in each game, so they stay with your account. Your rating and tier are shown to other players next to your username.

Coin purchases: If you buy coins, the payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple sends us a signed record of the purchase (the product, its price and currency, the store country and a transaction number) so we can add the coins, and tells us if a purchase is refunded.

RAMix+ subscription: If you subscribe to RAMix+, the payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple sends us a signed record of each payment (the plan, its price and currency, the store country, transaction numbers, and when it renews or ends) so we know until when RAMix+ is on, and tells us about renewals, cancellations and refunds. Whether you have RAMix+ (and until when) is visible to other players, since a subscriber’s name shows in gold on the daily challenge leaderboards; nothing else about the subscription is.

How you solved a board: The moves of your solve (not just their number) are kept with your result in daily challenges, duels and races, so other players who have played the same board can watch it.

Levels reached: The level you’re on in each game, shown to other players on your player page.

Messages: If you write to other players, the messages you send and receive. See Messages Between Players below.

Rewarded ads: Only when you choose to watch an ad for coins. See Rewarded Ads below.

Profile photo: Only if you choose to put one up. See Profile Photo below.

Country: The country you play from, shown as a flag next to your name. See Country Flag below.

Notification token: Only if you allow notifications, we store a token that lets us send notifications to your device, together with your app language, your current level and when you last opened the app. See Notifications below.

Prize contact details: Only if you win a tournament and choose to claim the prize, we collect your full name, mobile number and, optionally, your email address. See Tournament Prizes below.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your email address or phone number, except when you choose to provide them to claim a tournament prize
  • We do not collect your physical address
  • We do not collect your precise location; the only place information we keep is your country (see Country Flag). The ad services below receive your IP address, which can indicate your general area
  • We do not sell your data

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication, real-time database and notification services. Firebase processes data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

If you sign in with Apple or X (Twitter), those providers handle the authentication flow according to their own privacy policies.

Coin purchases and the RAMix+ subscription are processed by Apple through the App Store. Rewarded ads come from Google AdMob, and profile photos are checked by Google Cloud Vision (see below).

Advertising Attribution (AppsFlyer)

We use AppsFlyer to measure the performance of our ads, for example on TikTok.

  • If you allow tracking when iOS asks, AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • If you don't allow tracking, your IDFA is not shared, and our ads are measured through Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework instead.
  • In both cases AppsFlyer receives technical data such as device model, iOS version, IP address, and when the app was installed and opened, plus a few in-game milestones: finishing the tutorial, creating an account, and completing a level.
  • This data is shared with the ad network that showed you the ad, only to report that ad's results. We do not sell your data.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. AppsFlyer processes this data under its own Privacy Policy.

Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob)

RAMix shows an ad only when you tap to watch one in exchange for coins; there are no ads in between games. Those ads come from Google AdMob.

  • When you watch one, Google receives technical data such as device model, iOS version and IP address, and your advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking when iOS asked. Google may use it to choose the ad and to measure it.
  • To pay the coins, the ad carries your RAMix user ID back to our server, which confirms with Google that the ad was watched.

Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. You can change your tracking choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Profile Photo

You can choose to put up a profile photo, which other players see next to your name in matches, challenges and leaderboards.

  • Checked first: before it is saved, the photo is checked automatically by Google Cloud Vision for adult or violent content; Google does not keep it. A photo that fails the check is not saved.
  • Who sees it: the photo is stored, made small, with your account in Google Firebase, and only signed-in RAMix players can see it.
  • Reports: other players can report a photo; a reported photo may be hidden and reviewed by us, and removed if it breaks the rules. Players can also hide anyone's photo on their own phone.
  • Deleting it: at any time from the same screen; it is also deleted with your account.

Messages Between Players

Players with an account (signed in with Apple or X) can send each other text messages; pictures can’t be sent.

  • What we keep: each message’s text, who sent it and when, the two players’ names, and how many messages are unread, stored with the conversation in Google Firebase. We also count how many messages and new conversations you start each day, to apply the daily limits.
  • Checked first: every message is checked automatically by our server before it is delivered. Messages with foul language, or with phone numbers, links, email addresses or accounts on other apps, are not sent — this keeps conversations inside RAMix, where they can be reported.
  • Who sees them: only you and the player you are writing to. We don’t read conversations; when a player reports a message, we see that message and who sent it, to review it.
  • Notifications: if the other player allowed notifications, they get one with your name and your message.
  • Your controls: you can block a player, report a message, or turn messages off entirely in Settings (“Messages from players”). A player whose messages are reported by several players can no longer send messages.
  • Deleting them: when you delete your account, your conversations are deleted with it, for both players.

Country Flag

Other players see your country's flag next to your name in matches, challenges and leaderboards.

  • Where it comes from: our server reads the country of your internet address as Google Cloud reports it, and in some cases, such as a VPN, your phone's region setting instead.
  • What we keep: only the country, with your account in Google Firebase. We never keep your IP address or city.
  • Hiding it: at any time in Settings ("Show my country's flag"); it is also deleted with your account.

Tournament Prizes

When you win a tournament, the app asks whether you want to claim the prize. If you do, you enter your full name, mobile number and, optionally, your email address, and you must explicitly agree before they are sent.

  • Who sees them: only you and the RAMix team. With your agreement, we share them with the organizer named in that tournament, solely so they can deliver your prize.
  • What they are used for: contacting you about that prize. Nothing else — no marketing, and they are never sold.
  • How long we keep them: we delete them within 90 days after the tournament ends, or earlier when you delete your account.

Notifications

RAMix asks once whether it may send you notifications. If you allow them:

  • Reminders: if you haven't played for a while, the app schedules a few reminders on your device, for example “Level 3 is waiting for you”. They are created on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us.
  • Announcements: to tell you about things like new tournaments, we store your device's notification token with your app language, current level and when you last opened the app, so each message arrives in your language and only goes to players it fits. Messages are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push notification service.
  • Turning them off: at any time in Settings → Notifications → RAMix. Your token is deleted when you delete your account, and tokens that stop working are removed.

Data Retention

Multiplayer game room data is temporary and may be deleted after games conclude. User profile data (username, avatar URL) is retained as long as your account exists. Coins, ratings, your profile photo and your country are kept as long as your account exists; messages are kept until either player in the conversation deletes their account, and reports while we review them; records of coin purchases are kept for accounting and refunds. Notification tokens are kept while notifications are allowed and your account exists. Prize contact details are deleted within 90 days after the tournament ends. Tournament results stay on that tournament's leaderboard; contact us to have yours removed.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account from within the app, which removes your profile, your coins, your ratings, your profile photo, your country, your conversations and message settings, your notification token and any prize contact details you submitted, or contact us at the email below to delete any other data. Guest accounts store no personal data beyond a notification token, if you allowed notifications. To delete advertising attribution data, contact us and we will forward the request to AppsFlyer.

Children's Privacy

RAMix does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not contain objectionable content: profile photos and messages are checked automatically and can be reported, messages can’t carry phone numbers, links or other apps’ accounts, and any player can be blocked.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
